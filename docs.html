The flow

  1. You make two versions of your coin: A and B (name, ticker, picture). Both pictures and metadata go to pump.fun's IPFS.
  2. You pick a free qubit on one of the chips.
  3. You open the box: BOX submits one job to IBM Quantum for that qubit.
  4. When IBM returns the measurement, 0 means A, 1 means B.
  5. Your wallet signs the launch of the winner on pump.fun, then one small record transaction.

The quantum part

Chips: ibm_fez, ibm_marrakesh (IBM Heron r2, 156 qubits each). IBM connection: ….

OPENQASM 3.0;
include "stdgates.inc";
bit[1] c;
rz(pi/2) $q;
sx $q;
rz(pi/2) $q;
c[0] = measure $q;

rz·sx·rz is a Hadamard gate written in the chip's native gates. It puts the qubit into an equal superposition of 0 and 1. The circuit runs as an IBM Sampler job with one shot on the exact physical qubit you claimed. Real hardware is not perfect: a qubit's readout error (shown on its page) slightly biases the result, and that's part of the physics.

Why it can't be rigged

  • The job is tagged at IBM, before it runs, with your wallet, your qubit and a SHA-256 fingerprint of each version (name | ticker | metadata link).
  • The launch transaction is only built for the version whose fingerprint matches the measured bit.
  • One opening per wallet, per qubit, per 24 hours: no re-rolling.
  • Every coin page re-checks its measurement against IBM and shows the job ID.

Your qubit

The launch transaction carries a memo box:v1:TICKER:chip:qubit and lists the BOX registry address (…), so every claim can be rebuilt from the chain. The first coin on a qubit holds it. Qubit pages show IBM's latest calibration: T1 (energy relaxation), T2 (dephasing), readout error.

The launch

Every coin is created with pump.fun's create_v2: quote mint $BOX (Custom Pairs) and Holder Rewards on, so 100% of creator fees go to holders. Dev buy: up to 1 SOL, bought right after through SOL → $BOX → coin.

The record

Right after the launch, the creator signs one memo: boxr:v1:mint:ibmJob:bit:otherTicker:otherMetadata. It puts the measurement and the coin that never existed on-chain. That's the Graveyard.
